## Sub-benchmark commentary

- **Confidence calibration (§8):** the ordering property holds — VERIFIED claims from fresh
  authoritative pairs score ≥ 85; single-source and contradicted claims are capped well below.
  We assert ordering, not a perfect probability model (spec §8, §39).
- **Freshness (§8, §14):** the domain-aware thresholds behave correctly across news (days),
  web/docs (months), papers (years), documents (judged on their own metadata date).
- **Provenance / cache (§17):** every cached/local/stale case is scored, and the `no_false_live`
  hard gate passes on all of them.
- **Diff (§10):** `_classify` correctly distinguishes STRENGTHENED/WEAKENED/CONTRADICTED/
  UNCHANGED. NEW/REMOVED (unmatched-claim categories produced only inside `diff_runs` over DB
  rows) are validated by the DB-backed integration tests (`test_research_diff.py`,
  `test_hardening.py::test_full_lifecycle_coherent`), not the pure-engine benchmark — noted so
  coverage is honest.
- **Monitoring significance (§12):** noise suppression and authoritative-change surfacing both
  correct; the CPU claim (**Stage-1 makes zero LLM calls**) is verified by
  `test_hardening.py::test_monitoring_stage1_makes_no_llm_calls`.
- **Entity resolution (§13):** conservative — no wrong merges; legitimate surface variants merge.

## Manual-review protocol (spec §41)

Automated scoring covers everything objectively judgeable (evidence, confidence, provenance,
diff, significance, resolution). **LLM-synthesis prose is not auto-graded.** Two recommendation
scenarios (`recommendation_001`, `recommendation_002`) carry a `manual_review` note; the
reviewer confirms a reversal/emergence is warranted by evidence rather than noise. This is
documented rather than automated because report prose is not reproducible (spec §21, §41).

## Reproducibility (spec §21)

Fully deterministic and offline: no network, no real Ollama (`OLLAMA_BASE_URL` pinned to an
unreachable address). `engine_config` (diff/monitor thresholds) is recorded in the result so a
future threshold change is visible. `test_benchmark.py` runs the benchmark under pytest and
asserts the thresholds, so **future milestones cannot silently regress** research quality.

## Known limitations / deferred (spec §11, §40)

1. LLM-synthesis prose quality is manually reviewed, not auto-scored (report text is not
   reproducible).
2. NEW/REMOVED diff categories and full DB-backed diff/graph/monitoring flows are validated by
   the pytest integration layer, not the pure-engine benchmark.
3. Live-web collection quality is out of scope for a reproducible benchmark (no fixed corpus);
   the benchmark exercises everything downstream of collection.
